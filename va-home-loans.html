Funding Fee Exemption

Disabled Veterans — This Could Save You Thousands

🎖️ VA Funding Fee Waiver for Qualifying Veterans

The VA funding fee typically ranges from 1.25% to 3.3% of the loan amount — on a $400,000 loan, that's up to $13,200. Qualifying veterans may have this fee waived entirely.

✅Service-connected disability: Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt from the funding fee.
✅Disability rating pending: Veterans who have a pending disability claim at the time of closing may also qualify for the exemption.
✅Surviving spouses: Surviving spouses of veterans who died in service or from a service-connected disability are exempt.
✅Purple Heart recipients: Active duty service members who have received a Purple Heart and close before or on their discharge date are exempt.

Tell Frank about your disability status when you apply — this exemption can save thousands and must be confirmed before closing.

Certificate of Eligibility

How to Get Your COE — Faster Than You Think

Your Certificate of Eligibility confirms your VA loan entitlement to the lender. Here's how to get it:

1

Let Frank Do It

The fastest option. Frank can request your COE directly through VA's automated system during the loan process — often in minutes.

2

VA eBenefits Portal

Apply online at va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/request-coe-form-26-1880. Create an account and request digitally.

3

By Mail

Complete VA Form 26-1880 and mail to your Regional VA Loan Center. Takes longer — use online or lender-assisted options when possible.

4

What You Need

Veterans: DD-214. Active duty: Statement of service. National Guard/Reserves: Discharge or service records depending on activation history.
