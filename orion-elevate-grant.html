What Is the Elevate Grant?

Money Toward Your Home That You Never Pay Back

Most down payment programs are second mortgages — you owe that money back eventually. The Elevate Grant is different. It's a true grant: funds applied to your down payment or closing costs, no repayment, no lien, no strings.

  • 🎁
    It's a Grant, Not a Loan Once applied to your transaction, the grant funds are yours. Nothing to repay — ever.
  • 🏠
    Works on Conventional & FHA Pair it with a 30-year fixed rate loan — either conventional or FHA depending on what fits your situation.
  • 📋
    No First-Time Buyer Requirement Moving up? Already owned before? You can still qualify. This grant is open to repeat buyers too.
  • ⚡
    Per County Conforming Loan Limits Apply Program is available within conforming loan limits for your county. Ask us what that number looks like for your area.

The Process

How the Elevate Grant Works

Five steps from "I think I might qualify" to keys in hand.

1
Apply & Pull Credit

We review your credit, income, and overall picture to confirm you meet the 620+ FICO requirement and figure out which grant tier fits you best.

2
Run Your Numbers

We calculate your grant amount (1%, 2%, or 3%) and show you exactly what your payment looks like at our below-market wholesale rate.

3
Complete Homebuyer Education

A HUD-approved online course is required — typically 6–8 hours and can be done from your couch. Simple, worthwhile, required.

4
Find Your Home & Go Under Contract

Shop with confidence knowing your grant is lined up. We work with your Realtor to keep the deal on track.

5
Close & Collect Your Keys

Grant funds are applied at closing. You walk away a homeowner — and you never owe that grant money back. That's the whole point.
