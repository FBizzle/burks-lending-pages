The Features That Make USDA One of the Best Programs Available

Best Feature
🏡

100% Financing — Zero Down

No down payment required on eligible rural and suburban properties. This is one of only two government programs offering true zero-down financing — and USDA has lower fees than VA.

Low FICO
💳

550 Minimum FICO Score

One of the lowest credit score thresholds available on any home loan program. Buyers who have been told their credit isn't good enough may still qualify for USDA financing.

DPA Available
💵

Down Payment Assistance on Top

Stack USDA with our DPA programs through USDA DPA for even more flexibility. Cover closing costs, get forgivable second liens, and maximize what buyers can do with zero savings.

Flexible Income
📄

Manual Underwrites Accepted

Don't fit inside an automated underwriting box? Manual underwrites are accepted with a Verification of Rent or 3 positive tradelines with 12 months of history.

Seller Friendly
🤝

6% Seller Concessions Allowed

Sellers can contribute up to 6% of the purchase price toward buyer closing costs. In the right negotiation this means truly zero out-of-pocket costs for the buyer.

Property Types
🏘️

Wide Range of Eligible Properties

Single family homes, condos, modular homes, PUDs, leaseholds, and rural properties all eligible. More buying options than most buyers realize in the Middle Tennessee market.
