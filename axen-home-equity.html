Why This Product Stands Out

Not Your Average HELOC

Most home equity lines are variable rate — your payment moves every time Prime moves. This product locks in a fixed rate at origination, giving you the predictability of a traditional loan with the flexibility of a line of credit.

🔒

Fixed Rate — Locked In

Your initial rate is fixed and fully amortizing. When rates drop, your payment on the original draw doesn't drop with them — but neither does it spike. You know exactly what you owe.

💵

Full Amount Funded at Closing

Unlike traditional draw HELOCs, this line is fully disbursed at origination. The cash hits your account. You're not waiting to pull from a line — it's already yours.

🔄

Redraw as You Pay Down

As you pay back the balance, you can redraw up to 100% of your credit limit during the draw period. The flexibility of a revolving line with the stability of a fixed rate.

📄

No Appraisal Required

AVM-based valuation. Completed in seconds. No appraiser. No scheduling. No waiting weeks for a value. That's how this closes in as few as five business days.

Do You Qualify?

Here's What We Look For

Tennessee homeowners have strong equity positions. If you've owned your home for a few years, there's a real chance you qualify right now.

📊

Credit Score

Primary residence minimum is 620 FICO. Secondary and investment properties require 680+. Higher score = better rate and higher CLTV access.

🏠

Home Equity

Up to 85% CLTV on primary residences for qualifying scores (660+). Up to 70% on secondary and investment properties.

💰

Income

W2, self-employed, rental income, retirement, and SSA all accepted. Max 50% DTI. Income verified automatically through linked accounts.

📋

Loan Amount

Lines from $25,000 to $400,000. Terms of 5, 10, 15, or 30 years. The full amount is funded at closing, not drawn over time.

🏡

Property Types

Single family, townhomes, condos, PUDs. Primary, second home, and investment properties. Must not have been purchased within the last 90 days.

⏱️

How Fast

Application approval in about 5 minutes. Funding in as few as 5 business days for primary residences after the 3-day rescission period.
