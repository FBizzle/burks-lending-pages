Income Documentation

Multiple Ways to Prove Your Income

No W-2? No problem. ITIN Non-QM accepts the widest range of income documentation in the market.

📄

Full Doc

Traditional W-2s, pay stubs, and tax returns accepted.

🏦

Bank Statements

12 or 24 months of personal or business bank statements.

📋

1-Year 1099

One year of 1099 income documentation accepted.

📊

P&L Statement

Profit and Loss statement for self-employed borrowers.

💼

Asset Depletion

Qualify using your assets when income documentation is limited.

✍️

WVOE

Written Verification of Employment accepted as income documentation.
