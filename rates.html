Why the Headline Isn't Your Rate

Three Things the News Leaves Out

It's built on a perfect borrower.

The big national averages assume 20% to 25% down and top-tier credit. That's not most of the families I sit across the table from.

The program changes everything.

FHA, VA, and USDA price differently than conventional. For a lot of buyers, the loan the headline ignores is the one that costs the least.

Compare the APR, not just the rate.

A low rate can hide points or mortgage insurance. APR shows the fuller cost, so that's what I put side by side for you.
