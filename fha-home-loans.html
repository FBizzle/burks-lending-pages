500 FICO — No FICO FHA Available

Most lenders stop at 580. We go to 500 on approved eligible files and offer No FICO FHA for borrowers with no credit score. Expert manual underwriting handles the complex files others decline.

Zero Out of Pocket
🎁

100% Gift Funds Allowed

The entire 3.5% down payment can come from a family gift. Combined with seller concessions covering closing costs — qualified buyers can close with nothing out of their own savings.

Family Help
👨‍👩‍👧

Non-Occupant Co-Borrower

A family member who will not live in the property can be added as a co-borrower to help the primary borrower qualify. A powerful tool for credit-challenged buyers with supportive family.

More Properties
🏡

Manufactured Homes Eligible

Singlewide and doublewide manufactured homes eligible with FICO scores from 580. FHA-approved condos, PUDs, rural properties, and 1-4 unit properties all qualify.

Down Payment Help
💵

FHA DPA — No Income Limits

Down Payment Assistance available on FHA loans with no income limits. Stack DPA with seller concessions and the path to homeownership requires little to nothing out of pocket.

Rate Relief
📉

Multiple Buydown Options

3/2/1, 2/1, 1/1, and 1/0 temporary rate buydowns available. Lower your rate in the early years of homeownership when cash flow matters most. ARM options also available.
