Questions I Hear Every Week

Build-to-rent FAQs

What is a one-time-close DSCR construction loan?

It's one loan that covers building an investment property and the long-term financing, with a single closing. Instead of qualifying on your personal income, you qualify on the finished property's projected rent.

Do I need tax returns or pay stubs to qualify?

No personal income documents are used to qualify. Credit, assets, reserves, your builder and the project itself still get a full review.

Does 75% LTV mean I bring 25% of my build budget?

Not necessarily. Loan-to-value is measured against the property's value, not your construction budget. Project cost, how your land is counted and the full program guidelines decide the actual loan and your cash in.

Can I use gift funds to build a rental?

Gift funds are permitted on this program, subject to its rules on who can give them, what they can cover and how they're documented. We'll walk through that before anybody writes a check.

Can I build a short-term rental?

Yes. Short-term rentals are allowed, subject to program requirements and local zoning, HOA and permit rules. Check those before the foundation, not after the furniture.

Can I build a duplex or fourplex?

Yes. Eligible property types include detached single-family, PUD, 2 to 4 units and modular construction.

Do I need land and a builder before I reach out?

No. It helps, but if you're still looking we'll build your plan and budget first so you shop with real numbers.
