Buyer Readiness Checklist

Most buyers know their price long before they know their cash. Work through these six steps before the home search picks up speed.

You don't need every answer before you start. You just need to know which questions to ask. Tick each item as you go. Nothing you check here is saved or sent anywhere.

1

Pick a payment you can live with

Not the most a lender might approve. The number that still lets you sleep at night.

2

Know your cash, not just your down payment

The down payment is one number. These are the others that show up at closing.

Down paymentThe part of the price you pay up front. The amount depends on the loan program.
Closing costsFees for the loan, title, appraisal, and recording.
Prepaid expensesHomeowners insurance and property taxes collected up front, plus interest for the rest of the month.
ReservesSavings some programs require you to have left over after closing.
Help that may applyDown payment assistance, gift funds, seller concessions, and lender credits, depending on the program and the buyer.
3

Gather your income documents

How you get paid decides what you'll be asked for. Requirements vary by program.

4

Check your credit before a lender does

Surprises are easier to fix in week one than the week before closing.

5

Set your timeline

6

Ask what's been reviewed

These four words get mixed up all the time. Here's what each one means in my process.

PrequalifiedWe've talked through income, debts, and assets. Nothing verified yet.
PreapprovedCredit and documents reviewed. Still conditional on the home, appraisal, and underwriting.
Clear to closeThe underwriter has signed off on the conditions.
FundedThe money has moved. Now we celebrate.
