The Process

From Application to Keys

Here's exactly how the DPA Advantage program works from start to close.

1
Connect With Frank & Pull Credit

We review your full picture — credit score, income, profession, and goals — to confirm which assistance tier makes sense for you.

2
Determine Your Eligibility Path

You qualify through income (≤140% AMI), profession (first responder, educator, medical, military, civil servant), first-time buyer status, or an underserved census tract location.

3
Complete Homebuyer Education

A HUD-approved online course is required before closing. Choose your own provider. Up to $100 is credited back at closing — we've got you covered.

4
Identify Your Property

Find the home. Full submissions require a property address — no TBD files on this program. Your Realtor and I work together to keep the deal tight.

5
Close With Assistance Applied

Your 2% or 3.5% assistance is applied at closing, reducing the cash you bring to the table. You walk out a homeowner.

💡
Homebuyer Education Credit

The DPA Advantage program requires a HUD-approved homebuyer education or pre-purchase counseling course. You pay for it upfront — and up to $100 is credited back to you at closing as a lender credit. Use Framework Homeownership or any HUD-approved provider of your choice.
